What we do

Panel replacements, Federal Pacific included.

Main panels and subpanels, taken out and replaced. New breakers, and every circuit labeled on tape before we leave.

Example job: we replaced a main panel and two Federal Pacific subpanels, added smoke detectors while we were there, and left the place clean.

When something stops working.

A switch, an outlet or a light that quit, or a breaker that won’t stay on. Call and tell us what you’re seeing, and we’ll say when we can be there.

Example job: a switch stopped working, the crew was there within two hours and had it fixed in twenty minutes.

Outlets and new circuits.

More outlets where you actually plug things in — box on the stud, cable back to the panel, its own breaker when the room needs one.

Lighting.

Recessed lights, LED strips under the cabinets and along the toe kicks, glass cabinets lit from inside.

Hardwired EV chargers.

A charger wired into the house on a circuit of its own, run from the panel to wherever you park. One of ours meant pulling cable from the basement up through the attic and back down into the garage.

Smoke detectors.

Hardwired detectors on their own circuit, usually added while the panel is open anyway. In the panel photo further up, that circuit is the breaker labeled SMOKES.
